# Approved claims about the product

The only factual claims customer-facing writing may make about the product. Anything else about
the product is left out, however likely it is to be true.

- A duplicate charge is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- A subscription can be cancelled at any time and stays active until the end of the paid period.
- Support answers email within one business day.
- An annual plan costs less per month than a monthly plan.
- Account data can be exported from the settings page.
